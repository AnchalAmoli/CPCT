# Project Statement: Cancer Care Tracker System

## Problem Statement

In cancer care, keeping track of patient details, assigned doctors, treatment dates, and physical changes can be confusing if managed manually. Important details—like sudden weight loss or missed treatment dates—can get overlooked without a clear system.

## Scope of Project

This project provides a simple command-line system to manage cancer patient records, link patients with their doctors, track weight and pain levels, and view upcoming treatment schedules.

## Target Users

- **Nurses & Hospital Staff**: To enter patient details, record daily vitals, and view reports.
- **Doctors & Oncologists**: To see assigned patients and check their treatment history.

## Key Features

1. **Patient & Doctor Setup**: Register doctors and enroll new patients with basic age and stage validation.
2. **Care Team Assignment**: Connect a patient directly to their primary doctor.
3. **Vitals & Notes Logging**: Record weight and pain levels (0-10 scale) with automatic warnings if weight drops significantly.
4. **Treatment Scheduling**: Add chemotherapy or therapy dates and track their status.
5. **Patient Reports**: Display a full summary report for any patient directly in the terminal.
